Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Urdu Writer handles and what changes when optional services such as feedback storage or advertising are enabled.

Current service status: Documents are stored in your browser. Supabase account sign-in and feedback storage are available when configured by the site operator. Google AdSense is not currently connected.

Information stored on your device

When you create or edit a document, its name, content, and last-updated time are stored in your browser’s local storage so the editor can reopen it on that device. This data is not sent to an Urdu Writer document server. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to access locally stored documents. Clearing browser/site storage can permanently remove them; export a backup if a document matters to you.

Files you select are read by your browser to open them in the editor. The current application does not upload those file contents to an Urdu Writer server.

Feedback and account forms

The feedback page can send a category, rating, message, optional email address, page URL, and submission time to the configured Supabase database. If the service is unavailable or not configured, the page reports that feedback could not be saved.

When Supabase Auth is configured, email/password sign-in and Google OAuth send authentication details to Supabase. The account profile may include your email, name, and avatar supplied by Google or during sign-up. Supabase processes this information under its own privacy terms; review the provider's privacy information before signing in. The website does not use account data to upload or sync your documents.

Fonts and hosting

This site requests fonts from Google Fonts and loads the Supabase client library from the jsDelivr CDN. Requests to these third-party services may disclose technical connection information, such as your IP address and browser request details, to those providers. Review the Google Privacy Policy and jsDelivr privacy information. The hosting provider may also process standard server and security logs.

Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense ads are not currently installed on this site, and this site has not added advertising cookies or ad-tracking code. If advertising is enabled later, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access information on a visitor’s browser, measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted by the visitor’s choices and applicable law, personalize advertising.

Before displaying ads, the operator will update this policy with the active advertising providers and data practices, provide any disclosures and choices required in each visitor’s region, and configure an appropriate consent solution where required. For information about Google’s advertising data and controls, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google My Ad Center.

Your choices and document safety

You can delete saved documents from their cards on the home page or clear this site’s browser storage. You may choose not to provide optional contact details in feedback. Do not use a shared device for sensitive writing unless you are comfortable with other users of that browser profile being able to access it.

Children and contact

Urdu Writer is a general writing tool, not a service designed to collect personal information from children. For privacy questions or requests, visit the Contact Us page.
