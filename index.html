How do I type in Urdu?

Urdu keyboard mapping is on by default: press English keyboard keys to enter Urdu letters immediately. Space only adds a word space. Turn Urdu off to type English. Read the Urdu typing guide.

Where are my documents saved?

Documents are saved in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded to an account or synced to another device. Export backups of important work because clearing browser/site data may remove saved documents.

Can I open a TXT file?

Yes. Choose Open a TXT file on this page, select your file, and review its text and line breaks in the editor.

How do I print or save my document as a PDF?

Choose Print to print the document, or open Download and choose PDF to download a PDF file directly to your device.

Is login or Google sign-in working?

Not yet. The login, sign-up, and Google sign-in controls are interface-only and do not create accounts or save the details entered.

How can I contact support?

Visit the Contact Us page for available support options. Feedback is not delivered or saved until online storage is configured.
